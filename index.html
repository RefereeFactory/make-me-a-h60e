How it works

  1. Write freely. Give your note a title, type your thoughts, and pick a colour that fits the mood.
  2. Keep it. Press Keep note and it is saved instantly to permanent cloud storage — no account needed.
  3. It remembers. Close the tab, switch devices, come back next week — your notes load automatically every time.
  4. Find anything. Use the search bar to filter by title or content as you type.
  5. Edit or let go. Tap the pencil to revise a note, or the bin to gently remove one you no longer need.
